Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MTG AI Commander: Verdict is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast LLC. Magic: The Gathering, MTG, Commander and all card names are trademarks of Wizards of the Coast LLC / Hasbro, Inc. This app is an independent fan-made project created as a utility for players.

MTG AI Commander: Verdict ("the app") respects your privacy. You can use it as a guest, without an account, or sign in with Google or Apple to keep your decks in your account. The app has no ads and does not track you. This document explains what data the app processes and how.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores on your device: • Decks you import or generate with AI. • Your AI Judge conversation history. • Your personal API keys (Google Gemini / OpenAI / Anthropic), kept only on this device, in the app's private storage and the system's secure storage. They are only used to call that provider directly. • Language, wallpaper and other preferences. In guest mode all your data stays on the device only. You can delete it by uninstalling the app.

2. Account (optional) — Firebase (Google LLC)

You can use the app without an account ("Continue as guest"). If you sign in with Google or Apple we use Firebase Authentication: the app never sees or stores your password. Firebase Authentication keeps your name if your Google or Apple account provides it. We keep one document per account in Firebase Firestore, identified by your user ID, containing: • Your email address (with Apple it can be a private relay address). • Your saved decks, so they survive reinstalling the app and come back when you sign in. • Your AI uses balance (only used by the app's shared AI service). Google may process this data under its own terms: policies.google.com/privacy

3. AI features

AI Judge, deck analysis, deck generation and the card scanner use the AI provider whose key you configure in Settings (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic). Requests go directly from your device to that provider, under your own agreement with them. Only what the request needs is sent: your question, the card list or, for the scanner, the photo of the card. When the app's shared AI service is available, requests made without your own key go through our server to Google Gemini; the server does not store them. Deck analyses may be cached anonymously (without any account identifier) so the same request is not repeated. Google Gemini: ai.google.dev/terms OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy Anthropic: anthropic.com/privacy

4. Camera and photos

Camera: the scanner takes a photo of the card and sends it to Google Gemini only to identify it. The photo is not saved on the device or on our servers. Photos: if you pick an image from your library as a player background in the Life Tracker, it is used only on your device and is never uploaded.

5. Anonymous community corrections (optional)

If you are signed in and enable "Share my corrections" in Settings, when you mark an AI response as incorrect (👎) an anonymous record is sent to Firestore containing: • The associated commander name. • The correction you wrote. • An anonymous device identifier (no name, email or personal data). It is off by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

6. Crash reports

If the app crashes, a technical report (error message, stack trace and date) may be sent to Firestore to help us fix it. It does not include your name, email or account ID.

7. Card data: Scryfall, EDHREC and Commander Spellbook

To look up cards and analyse decks we use public community services. They receive no personal data: • Scryfall (scryfall.com): oracle texts, costs and card images. Card names are sent. • EDHREC (edhrec.com): popularity statistics. The commander name is sent. • Commander Spellbook (commanderspellbook.com): combo detection. The card names in your deck are sent.

8. No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads, includes no advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

9. Your rights and deleting your data

With an account: Settings → Account → "Delete Account" permanently erases your Firestore document (email, decks, balance), all data on the device (decks, AI Judge history, API keys, preferences) and your Firebase Authentication account. As a guest: we store nothing outside your device; uninstalling the app deletes everything. Anonymous corrections and anonymously cached analyses are not linked to any account. To rectify or delete them, or for any other request, write to us at: support@mtgoracleai.com We respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you in the app about significant changes. Continuing to use the app after the notice means you accept the updated version.
