MTG AI Commander — Verdict

A free companion app for Commander (EDH), the 100-card format of Magic: The Gathering. It tells you what is wrong with your deck and how to fix it, answers rules questions mid-game, and counts life for the whole table.

Install it — App Store or Google Play

Free. No ads, no subscription, no account needed.

What makes the deck analysis different

Most deck advice on the internet is generic: play more ramp, play 36 lands, cut your weakest cards. This app works from your actual list, and in two separate steps:

  • Code does the counting. Lands, ramp, card draw, removal, interaction, mana curve and colour sources are computed by deterministic code, not guessed by a language model. So are the contradictions between what you said the deck wants to do and what the list actually does.
  • The AI reads cards, it does not remember them. Before any analysis, the exact current Oracle text of every card in the deck is fetched from Scryfall and handed to the model as data. That is why it quotes your cards correctly instead of inventing what they do — the usual failure mode when you ask a chatbot about a decklist.

You get a scored verdict per axis, concrete cuts and upgrades with the reason for each, and a chat where you can argue with it about your own deck.

What is in the app

Deck audit and deck chat

Import from Moxfield or Archidekt, or paste a list. Ramp, draw, removal, curve, mana base and win conditions each get a rating and an explanation, plus cuts and additions you can apply with one tap.

Rules assistant

Ask an interaction in plain language and get an answer grounded in the Comprehensive Rules and in the real text of the cards involved.

Combos

The combos already in your deck and the ones you are one card away from, using data from Commander Spellbook.

AI deck builder

A legal 100-card deck for any commander, with a bracket, a budget per card and a strategy you choose. Cards you own (My Collection, organised in folders) are preferred, never forced in.

Life tracker

A life counter for 2 to 6 players: life totals, commander damage per commander (partners included) by dragging from your seat to the player you hit, poison, energy, experience and rad counters, Monarch and Initiative, a per-turn timer or one clock for the whole game, undo, and a d20 roll to decide who starts. On a tablet it sits in the middle of the table.

Playtest and mulligan

Goldfish the deck and practise mulligan decisions before you sit down to play.

Questions people actually ask

Is there a free MTG deck analyzer?

Yes — this app. The deck audit, the rules assistant, the combo finder and the deck builder are free, and the AI runs on your own API key (Google Gemini has a free tier). There is no subscription and no ads.

Why do I need my own API key?

Because there is no middle layer: your questions go from your device straight to the AI provider you picked (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic), with your key, which stays on your device. A guide inside the app walks you through getting one in a couple of minutes. Everything that is not AI — life tracker, decks, combos, playtest, prices — works without a key.

Can ChatGPT or Claude analyse my Commander deck instead?

They can talk about it, but they answer from memory of cards, which is where the made-up rules text comes from, and they will not count your mana sources reliably. This app fetches the current Oracle text of every card in your list first and does the counting in code, then asks the model to interpret what the numbers mean.

What is the best Commander life counter app?

That depends on what you need, but a life counter is included here, free and without ads: 2 to 6 players, commander damage tracked per commander, every counter type Commander uses, Monarch and Initiative, two kinds of timer, and undo.

Does it work with Moxfield and Archidekt?

Yes. Paste a deck URL from either, or paste a plain decklist.

What languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, Portuguese and Catalan.

Is it available on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on the App Store and on Google Play. One link for both.
